4 Final Model Selection
Logistic Regression was selected as the final production candidate based on its overall cross-validation performance, particularly its accuracy, recall and F1-score, combined with test-set performance and interpretability.

5 Why Not KNN?
KNN had the highest cross-validation precision (82.71%) and ROC-AUC (88.35%), but its cross-validation recall was 71.92%, lower than Logistic Regression's 79.74%. Therefore, KNN is considered a strong alternative rather than the final model.

6 Why Not SVM?
SVM achieved a high test ROC-AUC of 96.25%, but its cross-validation accuracy, recall and F1-score were lower. Therefore, the single test-set ROC-AUC was not sufficient to select SVM as the final model.

7 Why Not Decision Tree?
Decision Tree had the weakest baseline performance, with 72.22% accuracy, 68.75% recall and 71.88% ROC-AUC.

8 Why Not Random Forest?
Random Forest performed reasonably well but was behind Logistic Regression and KNN on the important overall metrics.

9 Final Conclusion
Logistic Regression was selected as the final production candidate for this project. KNN remains a strong alternative. The final model should be considered a decision-support prototype rather than a clinically validated diagnostic system.
